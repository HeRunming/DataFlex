# ICLR 2027 submission requirements checked on 2026-08-19

Official pages checked:

- ICLR 2027 Author Guide
- ICLR 2027 Reviewer Guide
- ICLR 2027 AI Policy for Authors
- official ICLR 2027 LaTeX style archive

## Deadlines

- Abstract: **September 18, 2026 AOE**
- Full paper and supplementary material: **September 25, 2026 AOE**

The abstract must be genuine and informative because it is used for reviewer
bidding. New authors cannot be added after the abstract deadline. Author order
may change until the full-paper deadline.

## Format and anonymity

- Use the official ICLR 2027 LaTeX style without modifying it.
- Initial main text is at most **9 pages**.
- Rebuttal/camera-ready allowance becomes 10 pages.
- References do not count toward the page limit.
- Appendix pages are unlimited, but reviewers are not required to read them.
- The review is double blind. Author identity in either the main paper or
  supplementary material can cause desk rejection.
- An anonymized code archive or anonymous repository may be supplied.

## Required/recommended statements

- **AI use statement: required**, excluded from the page limit, at most one
  page.
- Reproducibility statement: strongly recommended, excluded from the page
  limit.
- Ethics statement: recommended when relevant, excluded from the page limit,
  at most one page.

ICLR 2027 asks authors to disclose LLM use. The official policy labels
methodology/experiment feedback and result interpretation as required-disclosure
tasks, while literature review, drafting, editing, coding, reference formatting,
paper structure, and title suggestions are recommended-disclosure tasks.

## Reviewer-facing standard

The official reviewer guide asks whether the work is clear, technically
correct, experimentally rigorous, reproducible, and novel. Significance does
not require a state-of-the-art result: a submission can contribute impactful
empirical knowledge. This supports the current positioning as a controlled
negative/sufficiency result, provided the claim remains proportional to the
evidence.

## Author/reviewer administrative checks

- No author may appear on more than 20 submissions.
- Each author may be on at most one paper for which no author is an eligible
  reciprocal reviewer.
- Authors on three or more submissions have additional reviewing obligations.
- Each submission generally needs at least one qualified author registered to
  review at least three papers; the official exemption applies if no author
  meets the qualification definition.
- If no author registers as a reviewer after notification, the paper risks desk
  rejection.

Check the final author list and OpenReview profiles well before September 18.
